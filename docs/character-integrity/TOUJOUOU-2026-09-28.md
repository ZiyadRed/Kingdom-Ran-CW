# Toujouou / 悼襄王 source and artwork

The [official September 28 announcement](https://www.kingdomran.jp/info/urlg_toujouou) previews UR/LG Toujouou for the September 30 version 8.7.0 maintenance. RanHQ records only the three Alliance Conquest skills from that notice; it does not import his normal battlefield skills.

## Stable identity

The newest available local decrypted master (`C:/kingdom_data/decrypted`, September 17, 2026) joins `mstUnitGenerals.characterId=223` to UR general `428` and LG general `429`. Both rows have `countryId=2` (Zhao), `attributeId=0` (Infantry), and zero in `belong1Id..belong3Id` (no game affiliation group). `MsgUnitGeneralRubyName.stbl[428]` and `[429]` both read `とうじょうおう`. The 2030 `publicTime` in that older master is a placeholder; the official notice supplies the September 30 release timing.

The same September 17 master still assigns Toujouou a generic placeholder Alliance Conquest profile. The three official CW skill names, Japanese descriptions, and structured effects are therefore recorded as `pending_source` in `data/source/cw_skills.map.json`, with null canonical skill and text IDs. Resolve those IDs only after a newer master contains the released skills.

## Finished LG card

The official page embeds a 213×300 thumbnail. Its [original PNG](https://s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/official-operation.s3.sand.okp.mobcast.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/06105922/LG.png) is 313×440 RGBA (SHA-256 `ebe5a044bc5911f7cedc31a01733879ab6a24e4193147f75d93ec9eac3c1cf5b`). An exact copy is retained at `docs/character-integrity/toujouou-official-313x440.png` for provenance and rollback. The September 17 local cache does not contain `cg201_i02` character art or icon.

At the owner's request, RanHQ displays an AI-enhanced enlargement of the official card. The built-in image generator produced a 1058×1487 RGBA restoration candidate (SHA-256 `51732c754ff3e6a101e625a29afcba468932a0b3c8865dcd20e3bb459e449271`). Its calligraphy changed, so the complete Japanese lettering area was restored from the official PNG before export. The original card also supplies the final alpha mask. The site assets follow the established 626×880 full-card and 320×450 thumbnail sizes: `public/persos/toujouou.webp` (SHA-256 `76a497a982b967d614fd7dc9306cc276fbcae978b73e1dd5628ecd19c41a77d8`) and `public/persos/thumbs/toujouou.webp` (SHA-256 `ae57852e45767a7ef642fb74f6a610f4510691d4eb9af5a9c1fb7a55db64519e`). This enhancement is presentation artwork, not evidence for game text or mechanics. RanHQ's existing `CharIcon` uses its banner crop until a real icon arrives.
